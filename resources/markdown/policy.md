This Privacy Policy explains how **BluePin Inc** ("BluePin", "we", "us", "our"), the company responsible for Dot.Forms, collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information when you use Dot.Forms and the wider Dot Ecosystem it connects to. It is written to align with South Africa's **Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA")**.

## 1. Who we are

BluePin Inc is the responsible party for the personal information described in this Policy. Our Information Officer can be reached at privacy@infodot.co.za for any question, request, or concern about your personal information.

## 2. Two kinds of personal information Dot.Forms handles

Dot.Forms plays two different roles depending on whose data is involved, and this Policy covers both:

- **Your account information**, if you sign up and build forms on Dot.Forms — here, BluePin is the **responsible party** and this Policy governs how we handle it.
- **Form response data**, if you fill out a form built by one of our teams — here, the **team that built the form** is the responsible party for what they collect from you, and BluePin acts as an **operator**, processing that data only on the team's instructions, to run the platform they use. If you've submitted a response to a Dot.Forms-hosted form and have a question about how that specific data is used, contact the team that published the form.

## 3. What we collect (account holders)

**Account information** — your name, email address, and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text), via your Dot Ecosystem account.

**Team and form data** — the teams you belong to, and the forms, fields, and versions you build, publish, or archive.

**Technical information** — IP address, browser and device information, and session activity, collected automatically for security and to keep you signed in.

## 4. What we process on a team's behalf (form respondents)

When you submit a response to a form built on Dot.Forms, we store the answers you provide, along with basic technical metadata (IP address, submission timestamp) used for spam and rate-limit protection. Some forms may also include an optional consent checkbox or quiz-style scoring, set up by the team that built the form. We process this data only as instructed by that team — we don't use it for our own marketing, and we don't sell it.

## 5. AI-assisted features

Dot.Forms offers an optional AI drafting assistant that can turn a plain-language description into a starting form structure. When a team has this feature configured, the description they type is sent to OpenAI's API to generate a suggested field structure; when it isn't configured, a non-AI, keyword-based fallback runs entirely on our own infrastructure instead. Submission analysis (completion-rate and sentiment summaries) is a statistical/keyword-based process and is **not** sent to any AI provider — it runs entirely on our own infrastructure.

## 6. Why we process your information

We process personal information to:

- create and maintain your account, and authenticate you when you sign in;
- let you sign in once and move between connected Dot Ecosystem platforms without re-entering your credentials;
- let teams build, publish, and manage forms, and collect and review responses to them;
- deliver submissions to the destinations a team configures (Slack, Zapier, Make, or a CRM webhook), when they set that up; and
- keep Dot.Forms secure and prevent spam and abuse.

## 7. Ecosystem single sign-on

When you use another Dot Ecosystem platform to sign in to Dot.Forms (or vice versa), a short-lived, single-use authentication token confirms who you are without exposing your password to the connected platform.

## 8. Who can see your information

Account and form-builder access is scoped to your team: every form belongs to a team, and every viewer, editor, or owner role is checked against team and per-form collaborator permissions before anything loads. Form response data is visible to the team that owns the form (and anyone they've granted a collaborator role to), and to any destination the team has configured to receive submissions (e.g. a Slack channel or CRM).

## 9. How long we keep your information

We keep your account, team, and form data for as long as your account or the relevant form is active. If you delete your account, or a team deletes a form, the associated data is removed, except where we're required by law to retain certain records for longer, or where a team has exported and retained a copy separately.

## 10. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including encrypted password storage, team- and role-scoped access control, spam/rate-limit protection on public forms, and single-use SSO tokens for ecosystem sign-in. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

## 11. Your rights under POPIA

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

- request access to the personal information we hold about you;
- request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
- request deletion of your personal information;
- object to our processing of your personal information in certain circumstances; and
- lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

If your request concerns a form response you submitted, we may need to direct you to the team that owns the form, since they are the responsible party for that data. To exercise any of these rights regarding your Dot.Forms account, contact privacy@infodot.co.za.

## 12. Cookies

Dot.Forms uses a session cookie to keep you signed in. See our Cookie Policy for details.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you directly.
