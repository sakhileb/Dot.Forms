This Cookie Policy explains how **BluePin Inc** ("BluePin", "we", "us", "our") uses cookies on Dot.Forms.

## 1. What cookies we use

Dot.Forms uses one essential cookie, `laravel-session`, to keep you signed in and to protect your session against cross-site request forgery. This cookie is strictly necessary for Dot.Forms to work — without it, you'd need to sign in again on every page.

We don't use advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics cookies.

## 2. Why we use it

The `laravel-session` cookie lets Dot.Forms recognise you as you move between pages after signing in, and helps protect the authenticity of requests you submit (form edits, publishing actions, etc.).

## 3. Cookies on public forms

If you fill out a form published by a Dot.Forms team, submitting it does not itself set any cookie on your device — spam and rate-limit protection on public forms is handled server-side, not via a tracking cookie.

## 4. How long it lasts

The session cookie lasts for the duration of your browsing session, or longer if you select "Remember me" when signing in, per your browser's session-cookie handling.

## 5. Managing cookies

Because this cookie is essential to signing in and using Dot.Forms, there's no in-app way to disable it while remaining signed in. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings, but doing so will prevent you from staying signed in to Dot.Forms.

## 6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above.
